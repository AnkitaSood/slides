This is the flip side of the previous slide: instead of AI helping you build the app, the app itself is directly usable by an AI agent.

Contrast with a DOM-navigation agent: today, most "AI browses your site" demos click through a wizard. WebMCP skips that — the agent calls `submitSurrenderRequest` as a tool, same as any other MCP tool.

Be explicit that this is experimental — Angular's docs call the underlying WebMCP spec "very early in its lifecycle," so frame it as a preview of where things are headed, not a today's-best-practice.

Reference: https://angular.dev/ai/webmcp
