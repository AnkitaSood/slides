AI prompts often become structured input: instructions, constraints, user data, model options, and tool permissions.

Walk the real surrender form: one typed signal model, a field tree, centralized validators, and one submit contract.

The spicy part is `experimentalWebMcpTool`: plushelter derives an agent-callable tool from the same form humans use. Be explicit that this WebMCP integration is experimental even though Signal Forms themselves are stable in Angular v22.

Reference: https://angular.dev/guide/forms/signals/overview
